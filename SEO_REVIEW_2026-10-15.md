It is not a complete backlink inventory, does not prove there are no links, and does not justify inventing a link count, Domain Authority, Domain Rating or referring-domain score. Earlier Google/Bing/Maps observations are recorded in the public audit; they were not repeated unnecessarily here.

Links from Ram Realty to developer brochures are **outbound references**. Links between its own pages are **internal links**. Neither is an inbound backlink from another site. ## Official guidance used

- [Google-supported metadata](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/special-tags): meta keywords have no effect on Google indexing/ranking.
- [Title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link): concise descriptive titles, page-specific meaning and no keyword stuffing.
- [Snippets/descriptions](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet): relevant descriptions, with Google choosing how to display snippets.
- [Crawlable links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable): useful descriptive link text and crawlable destinations.
- [Site names](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/site-names): homepage WebSite name/URL data expresses a preference, not a guaranteed display result.
- [Link spam policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam): avoid manipulative links and paid link schemes.
